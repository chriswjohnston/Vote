A record of my campaigns for a seat on Nipissing Township Council, in the order they happened.

Running history

  1. May 18, 2021
    Vacant seat application

    Ten applications. The final choice came down to a name draw between Steve Kirkey and me, and it went his way. Read the news report.

  2. October 24, 2022
    General election

    Due to a death in the family in August, I did not run the campaign I had planned. I received 73 votes among nine candidates for four seats. Official results.

  3. May 9, 2024
    Vacant seat application

    Eight candidate applications; James Scott was appointed. Council minutes (PDF).

  4. December 19, 2024
    Vacant seat application

    Six candidate applications; Lisa Chalapenko was appointed. Council minutes (PDF).

  5. October 26, 2026
    General election

    I received 247 votes among seven candidates for four seats. The 2026 campaign site includes the governance record and platform. Official results.
