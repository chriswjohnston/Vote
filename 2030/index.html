The framework

From how Council governs to what Council is trying to accomplish.

The 2026 campaign asked how Council governs. The 2030 campaign asks what Council is working toward: the outcomes the Township wants, the resources needed to reach them, and how progress is measured.

01

Define the future

What should Nipissing look like in 10, 20, or 30 years? Rural development, agricultural preservation, infrastructure, property taxation, municipal facilities, and attracting new residents all belong in that conversation.

02

Make deliberate decisions

How should Council evaluate proposals, priorities, costs, and trade-offs? Decisions should connect to the direction the Township has chosen, not just the deadline in front of it.

03

Measure the results

How will residents know whether the municipality is moving toward its stated goals? Goals should come with measures residents can follow.
